Features

Everything a run needs, and nothing it doesn't.

Made to be read from a stride away and tapped mid-run: big numbers, big buttons, one thing at a time.

Drives the motor

Speed, incline, start, pause and stop, straight to the treadmill's motor board. The console's own speed and incline keys still work, and STOP and the safety key always win.

Workouts

Quick starts at a walk, jog, run or a 10% incline, and programs: intervals you can adjust, a hill climb, a steady tempo. A 3-2-1 countdown, then the belt follows the plan.

Any Bluetooth strap, or an Apple Watch through a broadcast app. Your zone lights the screen; the summary shows time in each.

How it works

An app on the treadmill, talking to the motor.

The console is an Android tablet. C1250 Deck runs on it in place of iFit's app, and talks to the motor board the way iFit does.

USB · ICON 213c:0002

The motor board

The board under the deck runs the belt and the incline. It's a USB device that speaks ICON's FitPro protocol, and C1250 Deck speaks it the way iFit does.

  • Outspeed, incline, mode
  • Backbelt state, Start key
  • Every250 ms

Safety stays in hardware

The console's STOP key and safety key stop the belt on their own. Nothing in the app can override them.
