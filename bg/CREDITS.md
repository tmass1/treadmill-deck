# Home backgrounds

Free to use under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Cropped to 16:10 at 1600×1000 and given one cool grade
(saturation down, cooler white balance, lifted shadows) so they read as a set. Each `*-blur.webp` is a pre-blurred twin that
the frosted cards sit on, since the console's WebView can't afford a live backdrop blur.
