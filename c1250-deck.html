    To check distance: put a strip of tape across the belt, measure the belt's full length (or look it up: the Commercial 1250's belt is in the manual), then walk at 3.0 mph and count the times the tape passes in one minute.     Connect your C1250

    1. Install QZ Fitness on your phone and pair it with the treadmill. QZ re-broadcasts the belt as a standard Bluetooth fitness machine.
    2. Open this page in Chrome on an Android tablet or a laptop, from its own https address. Safari and in-app previews block Bluetooth.
    3. Open Settings and tap Connect beside Treadmill, then pick the QZ device.
    4. Keep the safety key clipped on. Pause here is a convenience, not a replacement.
